REGIONAL VIEWER DESIGN

This viewer extends the Fire Shift Calendar prototype:
https://magenta-monstera-242d04.netlify.app/#panorama

The established identity is preserved: dark navy surfaces, Inter typography,
orange MODIS marks and cyan VIIRS marks. This viewer does not replace the
separate FuegoTrazo application hosted on Netlify.

Colors:
  background #091525
  panel #10243b
  surface #183552
  text #f4f7fb
  MODIS #fb923c
  VIIRS #67d8f2

Inter is self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License.
The comparison chart is primary; the department map is a secondary selector.
Missing values remain gaps. Weekly resolution and CSV provenance are explicit.
Changing filters does not change the fitted model or recalibrate probabilities.

On mobile, the layout becomes one column. Tables, native controls, visible focus
and keyboard navigation remain available. Coordinate lookup, partial date ranges,
missing values, exports and horizontal overflow were checked in the browser.

The historical-evidence section uses the same typography, surfaces and ruled
tables. It provides full denominators, source links, matched and unmatched records,
and native disclosures for detailed evidence and methodological limits.

See DESIGN.md for the extracted visual system and docs/VALIDACION_WEB.md for
the scientific interpretation of the evidence catalog.
